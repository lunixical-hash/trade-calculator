Privacy Policy

Lunix's AI Trade Assistant · Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lunix's AI Trade Assistant (“the Site”, hosted at lunixical-hash.github.io/trade-calculator) handles information when you use the Murder Mystery 2 trade calculator and optional Roblox sign-in.

Information we collect

If you choose Sign in with Roblox, we receive from Roblox (with your approval) basic profile information such as:

  • Roblox user ID
  • Username and display name
  • Profile URL and avatar image URL

We request only the openid and profile scopes. We do not receive your Roblox password.

How we use information

  • Show your connected Roblox identity on the calculator
  • Keep calculator data (such as My Items) separated per linked account in your browser

Where data is stored

Connected account details and calculator preferences are stored in your browser’s localStorage on your device. The Site is a static GitHub Pages app and does not run its own account database.

Third parties

  • Roblox — authentication via Roblox OAuth / App permissions
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static website files
  • Supreme Values — item value reference data used by the calculator

Cookies and tracking

The Site does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

Children

The Site is intended for users who are allowed to use Roblox under Roblox’s own terms. Do not use Sign in with Roblox if you are not permitted to authorize third-party apps.

Your choices

You can disconnect inside the calculator, clear site data in your browser, or revoke access under Roblox Settings → App permissions.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
